Understanding Your Results

NOI (Net Operating Income)

The property's annual income after operating expenses and before financing costs.

Cap Rate

A common metric used to compare the income potential of commercial properties.

Cash Flow

The estimated annual income remaining after operating expenses and entered debt service.

ROI (Return on Investment)

An estimate of annual cash flow compared with the down payment entered.
