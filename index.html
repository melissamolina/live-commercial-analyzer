BEYOND THE CALCULATOR

What Still Needs to Be Verified

The analyzer provides an initial financial screen. A commercial acquisition still requires property-specific review before a buyer can make an informed decision.

01

Income & Lease Information

Confirm rent, collections, lease terms, expirations, reimbursements and tenant concentration.

02

Expenses, Market & Property

Validate operating expenses, comparable data, property condition and relevant due-diligence information.

03

Offer & Acquisition Structure

Translate the analysis into pricing, offer terms, contingencies and an acquisition strategy.

04

Financing Structure

Compare leverage, debt service, lender terms and capital requirements against the property’s cash flow.
